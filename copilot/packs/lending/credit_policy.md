# Commercial Credit Policy (Synthetic, scaffold)

## 1 Debt service coverage

Debt service coverage ratio (DSCR) below 1.25x requires referral to credit
committee. DSCR below 1.00x must be declined.

## 2 Collateral

Loan-to-value above 80% requires referral with an appraisal less than 12 months old.

## 3 Leverage

Total debt to EBITDA above 4.0x requires referral.

## 4 Payment history

Any delinquency of 90 days or more in the last 24 months requires referral.
